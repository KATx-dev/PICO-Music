Privacy Policy for PICO Music

Last Updated: November 26, 2026

Privacy-First Approach: PICO Music is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information from our users.

1. Introduction

PICO Music ("we," "our," or "the app") is a free, open-source music player for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles data when you use it.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

PICO Music does not collect or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, advertising ID)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Listening history or playback statistics
  • Contact information
  • Any other personally identifiable information

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All app data is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to external servers:

  • Music Library: The app scans your device's local music files to create a playable library
  • Playback Statistics: Play counts, last played times, and favorites are stored locally
  • App Settings: Your preferences (theme, equalizer settings, etc.) are saved locally
  • Playlists: Custom playlists you create are stored only on your device
  • Downloaded Artwork: Album covers and artist images fetched from online sources are cached locally

4. Internet Access and Third-Party Services

PICO Music supports optional online streaming (via InnertubeX), lyrics/artwork enrichment, self-hosted music servers, and user-added BitChord-compatible extensions:

4.1 Optional Online Features (User Configurable)

  • Deezer API: To fetch album artwork and artist images (if enabled in settings)
  • LRCLib: To download synchronized lyrics for songs (if enabled in settings)
  • Spotify Search API: For enhanced metadata search (requires user-provided credentials)

4.2 Data Sent to Third-Party Services

When you enable online features, the following minimal data may be sent:

  • Song Metadata: Artist name, song title, album name (for artwork/lyrics lookup)
  • API Requests: Standard HTTP requests to retrieve images or lyrics

Important: No personal information is included in these requests. The data is only used to enhance your music listening experience.

4.3 Third-Party Privacy Policies

When you use optional online features, the following third-party services may be contacted:

  • Deezer: Deezer Privacy Policy
  • LRCLib: Open-source lyrics service with no tracking
  • Spotify: Spotify Privacy Policy (only if you configure your own API credentials)

5. Permissions Used by the App

PICO Music requests the following Android permissions:

  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: To access and play your local music files
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: To save downloaded artwork and playlists (Android 9 and below)
  • INTERNET: For optional online features (artwork, lyrics)
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: To play music in the background
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To display playback notifications (Android 13+)
  • WAKE_LOCK: To keep music playing when screen is off

All permissions are used solely for app functionality and not for data collection.

6. Children's Privacy

PICO Music does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app does not require any mandatory account creation or personal data collection.

7. Data Security

Local library metadata and preferences remain on your device. Remote stream URLs are resolved ephemerally and are not permanently stored. Third-party streaming extensions and online providers operate under their own privacy policies and should only be added from sources you trust.

8. Open Source and Transparency

PICO Music is open-source under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0-or-later), forked independently from Rhythm by Anjishnu Nandi.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be included in app releases. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Your Rights

PICO Music does not operate tracking servers or sell personal data. You retain local control to:

  • Clear playback history and ephemeral caches
  • Disable online music sources and third-party extensions
  • Export or delete your local playlists and preferences

All your local library data remains under your control on your device.

11. About & Upstream Attribution

PICO Music is an independent community project built upon upstream open-source music players and libraries, including:

  • Upstream Base: Rhythm (GPL-3.0-or-later)
  • Online Streaming Engine: InnertubeX (GPL-3.0)

12. Compliance

This app complies with:

  • Google Play Store Developer Program Policies
  • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
  • COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)
